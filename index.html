02 / MECHANICS
One loop. No exits.

 

  
01
Trade
The agent picks Solana tokens and trades them from his own wallet. Every swap is a public signature.

  

  
02
Realize
Closed positions in profit are booked. Losses stay on the record too. Nothing gets hidden.

  

  
03
TWAP
Profits aren't dumped in one candle. They're split into small buys spread over time.

  

  
04
$INVEST
Every slice buys $INVEST. The loop restarts with the trading stack intact.
